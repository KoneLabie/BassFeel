Get BassFeel-Setup.exe without installing anything (about 10 minutes)

1. Make a free account at github.com and click New repository (name: BassFeel, Private is fine).
2. Click "uploading an existing file" and drag in EVERYTHING from the unzipped BassFeel folder
   (include the hidden .github folder - on Windows, enable View > Show > Hidden items first).
   Click Commit changes.
3. Open the Actions tab > "Build Windows installer" > it starts automatically
   (or click Run workflow). Wait for the green check (~8-10 min).
4. Click the finished run > scroll to Artifacts > download "BassFeel-Installer".
5. Unzip it: BassFeel-Setup-0.1.0.exe is inside. Send that to your testers.

If the run turns red, open it, copy the error text, and send it to Claude to fix.
